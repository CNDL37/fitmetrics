## What we'd love to hear about

- **Corrections.** If you believe a formula, threshold, or citation on this site is inaccurate or out of date, tell us. Every calculation has a published basis, and we update the site when the evidence changes.
- **Feature requests.** Is there a metric or measurement you wish the calculator supported? Let us know.
- **General feedback.** Whether the tool helped you, confused you, or anything in between — your input shapes what we build next.

## A note on medical questions

FitMetrics provides educational estimates based on population-level research. We are not able to offer personal medical advice or interpret your individual results. For anything related to your health, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
