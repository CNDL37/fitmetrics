## No Personal Data Collection

FitMetrics is a **client-side tool**. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter into the calculator — age, weight, height, waist, hip, and neck circumference — are never transmitted to our servers and are never stored anywhere.

We do not create accounts, store user data, or collect any personally identifiable information through the calculator.
